## How these prompts are written

- **Call every input by its @name.** On the API, references are numbered by their position in the request: @image_1 and @image_2 for image_urls, @video_1 for video_urls, @first_frame, @keyframe_1 and @last_frame for frames, and @Name for a subject in elements or a voice in voices. Every mention has to match an input in the same request, or the request is rejected before anything is charged.
- **Turn "the supplied image" into a mention.** Many prompts from fal.ai's guide say things like "Use Video 1 for the camera movement and the supplied images for the scene", and they only work with those files attached. On the API, pass the video in video_urls and the images in image_urls, then rewrite the references as @video_1 and @image_1 in the same order.
- **Lock a character as a named subject.** Instead of describing the same face in every sentence, send it once in elements: 2 to 4 views with the main one first, or one 3 to 8 second single-shot clip of the person. Then write @Mia every time she appears. It does at the request level what long prompts here try to do with "the same person throughout".
- **Script the sound, since it is always on.** Sound cannot be turned off on Kling 4.0, so the prompts that land write it out: the kling4.video examples end with a "Sound:" line, and dialogue is quoted with the language named, as in says in English: "Sorry, was that oat milk or almond?" To give a character a set voice, bind a clip with voices on Kling 4.0 or elements[].voice_url on Flash. With reference videos, keep_original_sound keeps their own audio.
- **Write timed shots as text, and make them add up.** A common pattern here is "Shot 1 (0-5s): … Shot 2 (5-10s): …" inside one prompt. The API has no separate storyboard field, so those shots travel as plain prompt text: give each one a single action, and make the time ranges add up to the duration you request, 3 to 30 seconds (up to 20 on Flash).
- **Move format lines into the request.** Long X prompts often open with "Format: 16:9, 30 seconds, 24fps, 1080p", and a few ask for 4K. On the API those are settings: duration, resolution (720p or 1080p; 4K is not available) and aspect_ratio (auto, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 21:9). Set them in the request so the text and the settings do not disagree.
- **Keyframes for a planned path, references for a look.** Keyframes fix where the shot goes: a first frame, up to 8 middle frames and a last frame, with the total capped by the duration, from 4 frames at 3 seconds to 10 from 9 seconds up. References fix what things look like. You cannot send both in one request, and Flash takes a first frame only.

## Running these prompts through the API

The clips linked here are what each author got in the tool they used, not runs on our API. On apimodels.app the model is [Kling 4.0](https://apimodels.app/models/kling-4.0) (model id `kling-4.0`, [API docs](https://apimodels.app/docs/kling-4-0)), with a lighter tier, [Kling 4.0 Flash](https://apimodels.app/models/kling-4.0-flash) (`kling-4.0-flash`), on the same endpoint and request shape:

- One model id covers text-to-video; reference-to-video with up to 10 images, 5 videos, 7 named subjects and 3 voices that the prompt calls by @mention; and keyframe-to-video from a first frame, up to 8 middle frames and a last frame. Frames and references cannot be sent in the same request.
- Clips run 3 to 30 seconds at 720p or 1080p, and a prompt can be up to 10,000 characters. Length, resolution and aspect ratio (auto, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 21:9) are request settings, so a "Format: 16:9, 30 seconds, 1080p" line at the top of a prompt belongs in the request instead.
- Without video input it costs $0.157 per second at 720p and $0.235 at 1080p: 5 seconds at 720p is $0.785, 10 seconds at 1080p $2.35 and 30 seconds at 720p $4.71. With a reference video it is $0.094 or $0.141 per billed second, where the billed seconds are the output length × 2 when the input video is no longer than the output, or input + output when it is longer, so 10 seconds at 720p with a 5-second reference video is $1.88. Reference images and keyframes are not billed separately, and failed generations are not charged.
- Kling 4.0 Flash is 720p only, 3 to 20 seconds, prompts up to 8,000 characters, with a first frame as its only keyframe and no standalone voices list, at $0.094 per second (5 seconds $0.47, 10 seconds $0.94), or $0.063 per billed second with a reference video.
- Sound is always generated and cannot be turned off. There is no negative-prompt field, no multi-shot storyboard mode, no video extension and no 4K, so prompts written around those features have to be rewritten as one plain prompt.
- Every @mention must match an input in the same request, or the request is rejected before anything is charged. Prompts here that say "the supplied image" or "Video 1" need your own files in `image_urls`, `video_urls` or `elements`. Only upload faces and voices you have the rights to use.
- 16 prompts here are longer than 8,000 characters and do not fit Flash; 10 of them are longer than 10,000 and have to be shortened for Kling 4.0 too. The longest is 22,008 characters.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.apimodels.app/v1/video/generations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "kling-4.0",
    "prompt": "An exploration ship maneuvers through a vast cloud of charged gas and floating debris. Inside the cockpit, the pilot moves the controls while the captain braces against the console and an alien crew member monitors the storm. The camera moves from the crew toward the forward window, cinematic tension, realistic motion.",
    "duration": 10,
    "resolution": "720p",
    "aspect_ratio": "16:9"
  }'
# 10 s at 720p without video input: $1.57. The response carries a taskId; poll it until the state is "completed":
curl "https://api.apimodels.app/v1/video/generations?task_id=TASK_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
```

The prompt in this example is KL024 by @Alin_Reaper05, quoted as posted.

## How this collection was made

Prompts come from four kinds of source. Posts on X were found through searches and through three community collections on GitHub, [awesome-kling-4.0-prompts](https://github.com/tong0619453646-dev/awesome-kling-4.0-prompts), [awesome-kling-4-prompts](https://github.com/kingvid-ai/awesome-kling-4-prompts) and [awesome-kling-4-0-prompts](https://github.com/BravoNeo/awesome-kling-4-0-prompts). Every entry taken from a collection was checked against the original post on X: the prompt is the text in the post itself or in the author's own replies under it, and entries whose text could not be found in the post were left out, because those are usually translations or rewrites. The [kling4.video prompt library](https://kling4.video/prompts) and [fal.ai's Kling 4 prompting guide](https://fal.ai/learn/devs/kling-4-prompting-guide) are quoted from the pages themselves. Civitai entries are the prompt in the post's public generation data, kept only when that data lists Kling 4.0 and no LoRA or other add-on model.

Left out: posts that are not a usable prompt (ads, tutorials, news, comparisons of several models, chatter, fragments), promotions and referral codes, prompts mixed with a lot of commentary, sexual content, anything involving minors, real people's likenesses, graphic gore, prompts under 25 characters, and duplicates (the same post, or the same text posted again). On X, a label in front of the prompt such as "Prompt:", trailing hashtags and t.co media links are not part of the stored text; the prompt itself is not edited. Titles and categories are ours, and the IDs follow the order of the library on apimodels.app.
