Two shots. Shot 1 (0-5s): close-up through a train window, a woman in a camel coat presses her palm to the glass, eyes wet. Shot 2 (5-10s): reverse angle from inside the carriage, a man on the snowy platform walks alongside as the train starts moving, then stops and raises his hand.
