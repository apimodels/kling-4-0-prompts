## Prompt

````text
Begin from the supplied studio portrait and preserve the performer’s face, curly light-brown hair, light mustache and goatee, white ribbed tank top, tattoos, silver pendant, and black over-ear headphones. Create one continuous medium close-up of him delivering a focused rap performance. Keep the silver condenser microphone and black pop filter beside his face, without letting either drift across his mouth.
````
