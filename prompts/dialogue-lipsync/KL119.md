Text-to-video prompt:

A SINGLE CONTINUOUS 20-SECOND SHOT. No cuts.

SETTING: An extravagant medieval tax office, 1977 Eastern European live-action fantasy film aesthetic. Handmade brocade costumes, theatrical practical makeup, faded Eastmancolor photography, softly diffused sunlight, subtle 35mm grain.

CHARACTERS: Lord Bartholomew, an enormously pompous, stout nobleman with an elaborate moustache and gold rings, seated behind an oak desk. Opposite him stands a very tall, thin, elderly tax clerk holding a leather ledger. Both speak English in understated British accents.

0:00–0:03 — KEYFRAME 1: Medium-wide two-shot. A brilliant shaft of morning sunlight falls across the nobleman's desk. He points at the light and declares triumphantly, "Tax that!" The clerk freezes, quill raised.

0:03–0:07 — KEYFRAME 2: Camera slowly tracks toward the clerk as he studies the sunlight. He asks cautiously, "The sunshine, my lord?" The nobleman nods with tremendous satisfaction.

0:07–0:11 — KEYFRAME 3: The clerk steps toward the window while the camera tracks sideways with him. The nobleman announces, "Every last ray!" He folds his hands, pleased with his genius.

0:11–0:16 — KEYFRAME 4: Without cutting, the clerk closes the heavy wooden shutters. The sunlight disappears, and the room becomes noticeably darker. He turns back and says, "It's gone, sir."

0:16–0:20 — KEYFRAME 5: The camera gently moves closer to the nobleman, now sitting in gloom. His smile collapses. He shouts, "OPEN IT! THAT'S MINE!" The clerk slowly looks at the camera with weary resignation.

CAMERA: One uninterrupted, gently tracking camera movement at human eye height. Natural blocking and stable screen geography.

PERFORMANCE: Underplayed British comedy, microexpressions, perfectly timed hesitations. Physically consistent sunlight and shadows.

AUDIO: Realistic dialogue, creaking shutters, quill scratches, quiet room tone. No music, subtitles or artificial transitions.
