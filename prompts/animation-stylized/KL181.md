## Prompt

````text
Create an animated sequence of vibrant, text-based title cards, transitioning between a light cream and a solid blue background, evoking an uplifting and positive mood. The visual style should be playful, utilizing chunky, cartoon-like fonts with outlines and subtle drop shadows, along with simpler sans-serif text for secondary messages. Integrate animated graphic elements like sweeping lines, starbursts, hearts, and a progress bar.
````
