## Prompt

````text
Create a short animated sequence in a hand-drawn, highly stylized, glitch art aesthetic, featuring a menacing, shadowy figure against a vibrant, solid orange background. The figure should be black, with a textured, scribbled appearance. Begin with a full body shot of the figure (0:00-0:01), its glowing white eyes and sharp white teeth distorted by intense red, green, and blue digital glitch lines that constantly pulse and shift across its form. The camera is static in a medium shot showing the entire figure.
````
