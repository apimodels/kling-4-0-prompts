## Prompt

````text
SUBJECT
A young man with a short green crocodilian snout, scaled jaw, and bright green eyes, wearing a tan button-up shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, leans over a low wire fence in a backyard enclosure. He holds a small grey ceramic bowl filled with pale feed pellets. In the mulched pen beside a shallow stone-rimmed pond, two box turtles with dark, high-domed patterned shells wait.

ACTION
First he lowers the bowl onto the raked mulch and lets it sit. Then one box turtle lifts its head toward the feed and the other steps to the pond's edge. Finally he straightens, rests his hands on the fence rail, and watches the turtles with a calm, neutral expression.

SETTING
A fenced backyard habitat bordered by a wooden post fence and wire mesh, with two tall ornamental grasses at the corners. A small pond with lily pads and visible river stones sits in the foreground. Behind the fence, a green clapboard house with a white-railed porch and warm interior lights; a shaded wooden pavilion with string lights sits deeper in the yard. The mulch is freshly raked into neat lines.

COMPOSITION
A static wide shot from a low angle, the pond and turtles in the foreground, the boy at the fence in the mid-ground, the house and pavilion receding into soft focus. The frame never moves — no push in, no handheld, no zoom, no dolly.

LIGHTING AND STYLE
Warm low afternoon sun rakes through the trees, casting long soft shadows and a gentle back-lift on the scaled snout. Photorealistic, high-definition, with a subtle film grain and a candid documentary feel.

AUDIO
A soft outdoor room tone of rustling grass and distant birds as a continuous bed. Then the dull thud of the ceramic bowl setting on the mulch. Then a soft ripple as the turtle steps into the pond. No music, no dialogue.
````
