## Prompt

````text
Night in a narrow Osaka alley after heavy rain. Low tracking shot following a courier in a yellow raincoat who pushes a bicycle through puddles that mirror pink and teal neon signs. Steam drifts from a ramen shop vent. The camera slowly rises to reveal the tangle of overhead cables. Sound: rain dripping from awnings, a distant train, the bicycle chain ticking. Anamorphic look, shallow depth of field.
````
