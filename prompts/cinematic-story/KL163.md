## Prompt

````text
Create a steady wildlife-style close-up of a small dog in a rocky outdoor setting. The central subject is a small, tan and brown dog with a distinctive striped tail, akin to a raccoon's. The dog's face should have prominent dark eyes and perked ears, conveying an alert yet gentle demeanor, and its coat should be a mix of light tan and darker brown, with a noticeable black nose and whiskers. The setting is an outdoor, natural environment, characterized by an array of various sized light-colored rocks forming a loose, organic arrangement on dry, sparse grass and foliage. The lighting should be natural, suggesting a clear daytime shot, with soft shadows that enhance the texture of the rocks and the dog's fur.
````
